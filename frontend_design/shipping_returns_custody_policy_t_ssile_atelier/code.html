01. Dedicated Bolt Shipping & Cylindrical Packaging

To maintain the integrity of natural fibers—including pure Irish bast linens, unwashed raw cotton selvedge, and double-twisted Italian wool crepes—TÈSSILE Atelier maintains an absolute ban on folding finished textiles into parcels. Folding introduces severe fiber stress, irreversible cross-grain creases, and uneven tensile compression.

Every textile order exceeding two linear meters is meticulously rolled onto rigid, pH-neutral, acid-free recycled pulp cores. The roll is wrapped in archival mulberry tissue paper and housed within high-density double-walled cylindrical mailers sealed with reinforced kraft water-activated gummed tape.

02. Customs, Tariffs & International Delivery (DDP)

All dispatches destined for the European Union, the United Kingdom, the United States, Switzerland, and Japan are fulfilled on a Delivered Duty Paid (DDP) foundation. Clients encounter zero unexpected import processing surcharges, carrier brokerages, or local import VAT upon courier arrival.

All relevant textile tariffs, harmonized system codes (HS 5208, HS 5309, HS 5112), and municipal taxes are resolved directly through our Milan international logistics desk prior to courier transfer.

03. Fabric Inspection & Cut Textile Return Protocol

Sartorial Severance Policy
“Because yardage is measured and sheared from master bolts to your exact bespoke specification, custom cuts under 10 meters cannot be returned once shears have parted the weave, except in the event of a verified mill defect. Uncut full bolts may be returned within 30 days.”

We urge tailors and creators to utilize our Swatch Binder service prior to placing substantial yardage orders. Once a bolt has been unrolled and cut to an individual linear meterage request, its resale value as a continuous length is irrevocably diminished.

Full, untouched master bolts (typically 25 to 50 linear meters in original factory mill sleeve with intact selvedge lead seals) remain eligible for full exchange or credit within thirty calendar days of reception.

SECTION 04 Quality Assurance

04. Physical Defect & Lamp Inspection Guarantee

Prior to parcel dispatch, every single meter undergoes mechanical unrolling across our back-lit glass examination tables under calibrated 5000K daylight simulation lamps. Our guild inspectors mark and bypass natural yarn variations while actively preventing true weaving flaws (such as broken warp ends, heavy reed marks, oil grease spots, or mispicks).

In the exceptionally rare circumstance that an undetected loom anomaly reaches your studio, replacement yardage is dispatched immediately via DHL Next Day courier without waiting for return receipt of the faulted fabric.
