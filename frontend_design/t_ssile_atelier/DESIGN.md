## Brand & Style

The design system embodies quiet luxury, architectural stillness, and artisanal discipline—drawing equal inspiration from the tactile restraint of Japanese textile houses and the clinical sensory elegance of editorial apothecaries. It serves discerning patrons seeking bespoke garments, archival fabric curation, and made-to-measure tailoring.

The aesthetic philosophy centers on **Restrained Modernism and Tactile Warmth**:
- Generous breathing room and contemplative negative space evoke the tactile experience of unrolling raw linen across an untreated cedar cutting table.
- Interaction patterns are deliberate, silent, and unhurried; visual feedback relies on subtle hairline state shifts rather than aggressive animations or depth trickery.
- Typographic hierarchy pairs classical literary cadence with sharp, utilitarian geometric precision.

## Colors

The palette mimics raw paper, washed flax, tailoring chalk, and vegetable-tanned leather. Color is deployed with strict economy:

- **Canvas Background (`#FAFAF7`)**: Warm linen off-white providing an organic, non-glare foundation.
- **Surface Layer (`#FFFFFF`)**: Pure parchment white, used selectively for elevated panels, dialogs, and slide-over fitting rooms.
- **Primary Ink (`#1A1A1A`)**: Deep washed charcoal-black for core typography and primary call-to-actions.
- **Muted Ink (`#6B6B68`)**: Weathered slate-grey for metadata, secondary captions, dimension tables, and structural guides.
- **Hairline / Border (`#E8E6E0`)**: Tailor's chalk grey, used for structural division, thread-fine grid lines, and subtle containment.
- **Accent / Saddle (`#8B6F47`)**: Tailor's tan. Reserved exclusively for micro-accents, active swatch highlights, active navigation pips, and deliberate focus rings.
- **Functional Semantics**:
  - **Error / Sale (`#B33A3A`)**: Muted madder red. Never loud or neon; applied sparingly to archive markdown tags and form validations.
  - **Success / In-Stock (`#3F6B4A`)**: Deep moss green. Communicates atelier confirmation and atelier readiness.
- **Dark Mode / Archival Night (`#1C1A17`)**: Deep steeped sumi ink, used for immersive showcase contexts or nocturnal browsing.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy juxtaposes classical haute-couture editorial display with high-utility, balanced grotesque sans-serif elements:

- **Serif Voice (`EB Garamond`)**: Used for editorial titles, story introductions, and garments' poetic names. It carries relaxed, generous proportions with italic variants applied to fabric provenance and weave notes.
- **Grotesque Voice (`Hanken Grotesk`)**: Clean, objective, and quiet. Used for navigation, descriptions, sizing tools, and system states.
- **Eyebrows & Micro-Headers**: Rendered in strict uppercase at 11px (`0.6875rem`) with `0.2em` letter-spacing, colored in Muted Ink (`#6B6B68`).
- **Tabular Figures**: All pricing, thread densities, order totals, and measurement matrices must enforce `font-feature-settings: "tnum" 1` to maintain vertical alignment akin to tailor ledgers.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is architectural, balanced, and deliberately uncompressed. It relies on a 12-column layout grid on desktop with generous page margins to replicate open exhibition spaces.

- **Desktop (1200px+)**: 12 columns, 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters, 64px (`4rem`) outer margin. Maximum content boundary capped at 1440px to prevent unanchored drift.
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px)**: 8 columns, 20px gutters, 32px (`2rem`) outer margin.
- **Mobile (under 768px)**: 4 columns, 16px (`1rem`) gutters, 20px (`1.25rem`) outer margin.
- **Vertical Rhythm**: Section partitions should employ expansive vertical breathing room (`space-xl` / 64px minimum, scaling up to 120px between key collection narratives) to avoid consumerist density.
- **Structural Separation**: Sections and collection grids are divided by 1px solid hairline borders (`#E8E6E0`) that intersect edge-to-edge.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids physical drop shadows, blur washes, and skeuomorphic projection. Elevation is expressed entirely through **surface contrast, framing lines, and layered planes**:

- **Floor Level (Canvas)**: Background in `#FAFAF7`.
- **Sheet Level (Containers & Flyouts)**: Solid `#FFFFFF` surfaces bounded by a crisp 1px border (`#E8E6E0`).
- **Overlays (Fitting Modals & Fabric Viewers)**: Backdrop overlays use `#1A1A1A` with a 30% alpha fill without CSS blur filters, keeping the underlying page structure crisp yet subdued.
- **Focus & Interaction**: Elements do not lift on hover (`translateY`). State changes are signaled by border color transitions (from `#E8E6E0` to `#1A1A1A` or `#8B6F47`) or background shifts to `#F3F2ED`.

## Shapes

The geometry of the atelier is clean-cut, mirroring the precision of tailored seams and freshly sheared bolt edges:

- **Corner Radii**: Strictly sharp (`0px`). In rare instances where soft touch is necessary (such as isolated swatch pills or focus outlines), corner rounding must never exceed a micro-radius of `2px`.
- **Image Ratios**: Photography of silhouettes, drapes, and lookbooks strictly observes a vertical **4:5 aspect ratio**. Macro fabric close-ups and process details observe a **1:1 square ratio**.
- **Dividers & Strokes**: Stroke widths are locked to 1px hairline thickness (`border-width: 1px`).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action**: Solid Ink (`#1A1A1A`) fill, pure white (`#FFFFFF`) text, sharp 0px radius, 0.2em letter-spaced uppercase label. Hover shifts to an understated charcoal (`#2E2D2A`).
- **Secondary / Hairline**: Transparent fill, 1px border in `#1A1A1A` or `#E8E6E0`, `#1A1A1A` text. Hover shifts background to `#F3F2ED`.
- **Text Link**: Understated inline text accompanied by a hairline bottom border with 2px offset; shifts to Tailor's Saddle (`#8B6F47`) on hover.

### Inputs & Form Fields
- **Underline Input**: Borderless container with a single 1px solid bottom hairline (`#E8E6E0`). Focus transitions the bottom border to `#8B6F47` or `#1A1A1A` with no box-shadow ring.
- **Labels**: Fixed uppercase micro-label above the field (`label-eyebrow`), non-floating, rendered in `#6B6B68`.
- **Sensory Microcopy**: Helper text uses polite, tranquil phrasing (e.g., *"Specify measurements in centimeters, precisely taken across the shoulder yoke"*).

### Product & Fabric Cards
- **Structure**: 4:5 image container on `#FAFAF7` ground with 1px border framing.
- **Metadata**: Display title in serif (`title-lg`), fabric composition in muted eyebrow sans, and price formatted with tabular figures.
- **Fabric Swatch Selectors**: A horizontal array of 12px circular swatch dots, enclosed in a 1px border. The active selection features an outer 1px hairline ring with 2px offset in `#8B6F47`.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- **Checkboxes**: 14px square, 0px border radius, 1px hairline outline (`#6B6B68`). Active check displays a crisp, minimal checkmark in `#1A1A1A`.
- **Radio Buttons**: 14px circular hairline ring. Checked state displays a solid 6px concentric interior dot in `#1A1A1A`.

### Lists & Atelier Ledgers
- Minimalist ledger rows divided by full-width 1px hairline rules (`#E8E6E0`).
- Columns align title to the left, metadata centered, and measurements or prices flushed right with tabular numbers.

### Bespoke Brand Mark
- Centered, classical serif typographic logotype (`TÈSSILE Atelier` or `Atelier & Co.`) featuring expanded tracking (`0.25em`), balanced above a subtle hairline divider separating header and navigation.
