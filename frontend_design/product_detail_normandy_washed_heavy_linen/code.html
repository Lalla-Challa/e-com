• Grainline Discipline: Because of the natural springiness in washed linen yarns, cut all garment panels strictly on the straight grain using sharp rotary shears or shears freshly honed at 20 degrees.

• Seam Allowance: We advocate minimum 1.5 cm seam allowances. Finish with traditional Hong Kong bias binding using 8mm habotai silk or an English flat-felled seam to encapsulate the unraveled raw yarns gracefully.

  • water_drop Laundering: Machine wash cold at 30°C on a delicate cycle using mild pH-neutral ecological textile wash. Avoid chlorine agents, optical brighteners, and heavy spinning cycles (<600 RPM).
  • air Drying: Never tumble dry. Gently snap wet cloth lengthwise and dry suspended horizontally in shade. UV exposure will accelerate natural flax patina over decades.
  • iron Steam & Press: Press while fibers remain slightly damp with an iron set to high linen temperature. Utilize an untreated beechwood tailor's clapper immediately following high-pressure steam to lock sharp knife-edge creases into lapels or trouser pleats.
