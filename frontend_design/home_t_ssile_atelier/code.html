The Discipline

Éthique & Matière
“A garment holds only as much dignity as the cloth from which it was cut.”

We work exclusively with family-held weaving mills and historic button turners who refuse petroleum-synthesized treatments, prioritizing the honest mechanical drape of raw natural fibers.

01 / TRACEABILITY

Mill-Direct Origin

Every yard of wool, linen, and cotton is authenticated with mill provenance. From the specific sheep farms in Yorkshire to water retting fields in Normandy.

Verified Lot Documentation
02 / PURITY

Zero Micro-Synthetics

Pure silk threads, horn, tagua nut buttons, and 100% natural horsehair canvassing. We eliminate all synthetic polymers to ensure seamless steam responsiveness during tailoring.

100% Biodegradable Construction
03 / CONTINUITY

Permanent Stocking

Ateliers require replenishment stability. Our foundational suit cloths, basting threads, and pocketings are stocked continuously across multiple seasons.

Guaranteed Re-order Dye-Match
